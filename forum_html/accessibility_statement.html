Accessibility Statement

At EduVibe, we are committed to providing an inclusive and accessible learning experience for everyone, including individuals with disabilities. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA to ensure our platform is easy to navigate, understand, and use.

We strive to improve accessibility by providing consistent layouts, responsive designs across different devices, keyboard-friendly navigation, readable text with sufficient colour contrast, meaningful image descriptions, and accessible forms with clear feedback messages.

If you experience difficulties accessing any part of EduVibe or have suggestions for improving accessibility, please contact us at eduvibe.learning@gmail.com with "Accessibility" in the email subject.

Please include the following information where possible:

  • A brief description of the issue.
  • The page URL where the issue occurred.
  • The device and browser you are using.
  • Any assistive technology you are using, if applicable.

We value your feedback and will make reasonable efforts to address accessibility issues and continuously improve the learning experience for all users.
