Welcome to EduVibe!

At EduVibe ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

EduVibe is an online learning platform where users can explore and purchase technology-related courses, participate in community discussions, read educational blogs, save courses to their wishlists, and share reviews.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you access or use our website and services.

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Vietnamese laws, including the Law on Personal Data Protection (Law No. 91/2025/QH15).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use EduVibe.

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you register or interact with our platform, we may collect:

  • Full name and username
  • Email address
  • Account password
  • Profile picture (if provided)
  • Contact information voluntarily submitted
  • Information included in communications with our support team

1.2 Course and Learning Information

When you interact with courses, we may collect:

  • Courses you purchase or enrol in
  • Courses added to your wishlist or shopping cart
  • Course ratings and reviews
  • Learning preferences and interests
  • Course progress, if progress tracking is available

1.3 Community and User-Generated Content

When you participate in our Discussion Forum or other interactive features, we may collect:

  • Discussion titles and content
  • Replies and comments
  • Images uploaded to discussions
  • Ratings and reviews
  • Dates and times of posts
  • Saved discussions and other interactions

Please remember that information you choose to publish in public discussions or reviews may be visible to other users.

1.4 Information Collected Automatically

When you access our website, certain technical information may be collected, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited
  • Website usage and interaction data
  • Cookies or similar technologies, where implemented

This information helps us operate, secure, and improve our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for appropriate, disclosed purposes, including:

Account Management

To create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and provide access to EduVibe services.

Course Services

To manage course enrolments, purchases, wishlists, and learning activities.

Community Features

To allow users to create discussions, reply to posts, share reviews, and interact with other learners.

Website Improvement

To understand how users interact with EduVibe and improve website navigation, functionality, accessibility, and performance.

Customer Support

To respond to inquiries, resolve issues, and provide assistance.

Security and Legal Compliance

To prevent unauthorized access, detect suspicious activities, maintain platform security, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

We will inform users and obtain consent where required before using personal information for additional purposes.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

EduVibe does not sell users' personal information.

We may share limited personal information in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

When necessary to operate EduVibe, authorized providers such as website hosting or payment-processing services may process information on our behalf.

Public Community Features

Information voluntarily posted in public discussions, comments, or course reviews may be visible to other users.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid request from an authorized government authority.

With Your Consent

We may share information for other purposes when you have provided the necessary consent.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, appropriate privacy and security safeguards should be maintained.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

EduVibe may use cookies or similar browser technologies to support website functionality and improve user experience.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Remember user preferences
  • Support shopping cart functionality
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand how visitors use the platform

Users can manage or delete cookies through their browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

Where required by law, EduVibe will request consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We recognize the importance of keeping personal information secure.

EduVibe aims to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

These measures may include:

  • Secure authentication
  • Appropriate password protection
  • Access controls
  • Secure data transmission
  • Protection against unauthorized account access
  • Regular review of potential security risks

Although we take reasonable precautions, no online platform can guarantee complete security.

Users are encouraged to use strong passwords and keep their account credentials confidential.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable Vietnamese personal data protection laws, users may have rights concerning their personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Be informed: Understand how and why personal information is processed.
  • Give or withdraw consent: Control processing activities that rely on consent.
  • Access information: Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Correct information: Request updates to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete information: Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • Restrict or object to processing: Exercise applicable rights regarding the use of their personal information.
  • Raise concerns: Contact EduVibe about the handling of their personal information.

Certain rights may be subject to limitations or exceptions provided by law.

Users may contact us at eduvibe.learning@gmail.com to submit privacy-related requests.

7. Managing and Deactivating Your Account

Users may be able to manage their personal information through their EduVibe account settings.

Where available, account management features may include:

  • Updating profile details
  • Changing passwords
  • Managing saved courses and wishlists
  • Editing or deleting their own discussion content
  • Requesting account deactivation or deletion

Account deactivation and permanent account deletion are different actions.

Deactivation may temporarily restrict access to an account while certain data remains stored.

Permanent deletion may result in the removal of account information, subject to legal obligations and necessary retention requirements.

For example, some transaction records may need to be retained for legal or accounting purposes.

Information that must be retained will be protected and processed only for permitted purposes.

Users may contact eduvibe.learning@gmail.com for assistance with account or personal data requests.

8. How Long We Keep Your Information

EduVibe retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • Whether an account remains active
  • The purpose for which information was collected
  • Whether the user requests deletion
  • Legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements
  • Security and dispute-resolution needs

When personal information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymized, as appropriate.

9. Children's Privacy

EduVibe recognizes the importance of protecting children's personal information.

Where children are permitted to use EduVibe, their personal information will be handled in accordance with applicable Vietnamese laws, including relevant requirements for age verification and consent from a parent or legal guardian.

Children should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public discussions, reviews, or other community features.

Parents or legal guardians may contact eduvibe.learning@gmail.com with concerns or requests regarding a child's personal information.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

EduVibe may contain links to external educational resources, websites, or services.

These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

EduVibe is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

We encourage users to review the relevant privacy policies before providing personal information to external services.

If EduVibe uses external service providers that transfer personal information outside Vietnam, applicable requirements for international personal data transfers will be followed.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our platform, services, or applicable legal requirements.

When significant changes occur, we will take appropriate steps to inform users, such as updating the policy on our website or providing additional notice where necessary.

The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.

Where required by law, we will request additional consent before implementing relevant changes to personal data processing.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact:

EduVibe Support Team

Email: eduvibe.learning@gmail.com

We will review and respond to privacy-related requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
